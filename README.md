# Thinking in Public

Public learning, thinking, and writing archive by Pat (Napatchol Thaipanich).

This repo collects notes and posts about software engineering, AI, product thinking, design, events, and career growth. It is intentionally imperfect: some pieces are polished reflections, and some are raw notes captured while learning.

## Overview

`thinking-in-public` is a place to make learning visible. Instead of keeping every idea in private notes, this repo turns selected observations into a browsable archive that can show how my thinking changes over time.

The current content includes:

- structured posts from tech events and community sessions;
- raw notes from talks, courses, and tools I am learning;
- screenshots or supporting assets used by posts;
- reflections on engineering practice, AI-assisted development, and career growth.

## Why This Repo Exists

I realized I was learning a lot, but not recording how I think.

This repo exists to:

- build a habit of writing while learning;
- practice explaining technical ideas clearly;
- keep event notes and personal reflections searchable;
- turn rough observations into more useful posts over time;
- show learning momentum as part of a career profile.

This is not meant to be a polished publication archive only. It is a working space for honest learning, revision, and reflection.

## Writing Principles

- Write honestly before writing perfectly.
- Separate raw notes from polished posts.
- Keep the personal reflection visible, not only the technical summary.
- Link to sources or event references when they shaped the note.
- Improve old notes when a clearer explanation becomes available.
- Avoid pretending rough notes are final research.
- Do not invent attendance, results, expertise, or claims that are not supported by the content.

## Post Maturity Levels

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Raw note` | Captured during or soon after learning; may contain rough wording, incomplete structure, or open questions |
| `Draft` | Shaped into a readable flow but still needs revision, references, or stronger takeaways |
| `Published post` | Structured enough to share as a standalone reflection |
| `Evergreen` | Revised over time into a durable reference or guide |

Most files in `notes/` should be treated as `Raw note`. Files in `posts/` are closer to `Published post`, but may still evolve.
